Versus Thursdays are a monthly fighting game meetup to challenge other players in various fighting games and level up as a community.\
Casual matches and building an offline environment to play fighting games in Basel are the main focus.

Versus Thursdays happen once a month, on the **last Thursday** of the month.

Beginners are welcome!

## Details

* Location: [ManaBar Basel](https://manabar.ch/)
* Date: Last Thursday of the month
* Time: 19:00 - 23:00
* Venue fee: CHF 5.-
* Questions and updates in the HardRead Discord: <https://discord.gg/sB4VQbf85h>

We usually have 4-6 PC setups with a variety of games.

Games:\
Basically all popular fighting games. Join the HardRead Discord and ask in the Versus Thursdays channel (Feel free to ping me @okk) 😄

You are free to bring your own console/PC if you want to play a specific game.

Please note that this event is held in a bar. You are not allowed to bring your own drinks or food.\
Thank you.
